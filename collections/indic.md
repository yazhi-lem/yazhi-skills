# Collection — Indic & multicultural

A deliberately evolving side-collection for multilingual Indic and cross-cultural work. Tamil has its own mature category; `indic/` covers the cross-script and cross-language seams.

## Evolution rule

This collection is expected to grow. Every new language or cultural entry must have:

1. a native reviewer,
2. a source or reviewer note with a date,
3. an owner in `docs/README.md` or a living register, and
4. a boundary against an existing Tamil skill so there is one source of truth.

See [`yazhi-review`](../skills/yazhi-purpose/yazhi-review/SKILL.md) for the language-axis verdict before merge.
